## Typography — Instrument Serif + Inter + JetBrains Mono

- **Display:** Instrument Serif (headlines)
- **Body:** Inter
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono (dates and metadata)

Loaded from Google Fonts in `frontend/index.html`, with system fallbacks. The app layout gives Kazakh and Russian text fallbacks if a font subset is unavailable.

## Gradients

Both gradients are enabled and defined in `frontend/src/styles.css`.

- Background gradient: subtle warm paper tint for the hero illustration.
- Accent gradient: deep terracotta to muted brick, used for the decorative sun in the hero illustration.

No reading text is placed over either gradient. Main buttons use solid palette colors for clear contrast.

## Tone and voice

Use measured, human wording. Invite the reader to explore; state dates and uncertainty plainly. Keep questions easy to understand and place sources close to historical claims.

Avoid hype, exaggerated claims, childish jokes, and implying that a generated answer is verified scholarship.

**Voice example:** “Read a short explanation, then open the source and check the date.”

## Usage

- Use theme variables in the stylesheet rather than introducing one-off colors.
- Keep a 4 px spacing rhythm and body copy at 16 px or larger where practical.
- Keep links and buttons visibly focused and large enough for touch.
- Check light and dark themes and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Reserve the serif for display headings; keep long explanations in the sans font.
- Keep gradients decorative. Use solid backgrounds behind reading text and forms.
