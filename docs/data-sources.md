# Data sources

## Kazakhstan independence

- [Adilet, Kazakh text of the 1991 constitutional law](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/kaz/docs/Z910004400_): primary legal source.
- [U.S. Department of State, Office of the Historian](https://history.state.gov/countries/kazakhstan/1000): secondary corroboration for U.S. recognition on 25 December 1991, distinguished from the date of Kazakhstan's law.

## Ablai Khan

- [E-history.kz, Kazakh Khanate chronology](https://e-history.kz/ru/chronology/event/503): states that Ablai was proclaimed khan in 1771 and ruled from 1771 to 1781.
- [Government of North Kazakhstan Region, regional history](https://www.gov.kz/org/sko/press/article/details/1581): describes 1771–1781 as his period as khan of the three Kazakh zhuzes.

## Tanbaly archaeological landscape

- [UNESCO World Heritage Centre](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1145/): description of the archaeological landscape, petroglyphs, settlements, and burials.

## Additional historical events

- [E-history.kz: Establishment of the Kazakh Khanate](https://e-history.kz/en/amp/history-of-kazakhstan/show/9553): relates formation to Kerei and Zhanibek and reports 1465–1466 from the dating of Muhammad Haydar Dulati. The app preserves this as a range.
- [UNESCO: Mausoleum of Khoja Ahmed Yasawi](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1103/): states construction took place from 1389 to 1405 under Timur and remained incomplete.
- [Adilet: State Sovereignty Declaration](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/B900001700_): primary legal text for the 25 October 1990 act.
- [E-history.kz: December 1986 events](https://e-history.kz/kz/calendar/show/26108): dates the protests in Almaty to 17–18 December 1986.
- [State Archive of West Kazakhstan Region: decree No. 409](https://muragat-bko.gov.kz/ru/archive/catalog?delo=98101&doki=80473&fond=289&opis=1611): archival catalog for the 29 August 1991 closure of the Semipalatinsk test site.
- [United Nations in Kazakhstan](https://kazakhstan.un.org/kk/download/5340/27316): gives Kazakhstan's UN admission date as 2 March 1992.
- [National Bank of Kazakhstan: 1993 banknotes](https://nationalbank.kz/kz/news/banknoty/6787): records the tenge banknotes' introduction on 15 November 1993.

The seeded event text is a short, bilingual summary of these sources. It has not yet undergone professional historian or translator sign-off. User-imported document content is marked unreviewed and is kept separate from the seeded historical catalog.

English source metadata has been summarized into Kazakh and Russian in the seed records. Those translations and wording still need review by a subject-matter editor. The approximate chronology for Tanbaly is intentionally broad.
