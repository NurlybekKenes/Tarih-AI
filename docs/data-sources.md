# Data sources

## Kazakhstan independence

- [Adilet, Kazakh text of the 1991 constitutional law](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/kaz/docs/Z910004400_): primary legal source.
- [U.S. Department of State, Office of the Historian](https://history.state.gov/countries/kazakhstan/1000): secondary corroboration for U.S. recognition on 25 December 1991, distinguished from the date of Kazakhstan's law.

## Ablai Khan

- [E-history.kz, Kazakh Khanate chronology](https://e-history.kz/ru/chronology/event/503): states that Ablai was proclaimed khan in 1771 and ruled from 1771 to 1781.
- [Government of North Kazakhstan Region, regional history](https://www.gov.kz/org/sko/press/article/details/1581): describes 1771–1781 as his period as khan of the three Kazakh zhuzes.

## Tanbaly archaeological landscape

- [UNESCO World Heritage Centre](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1145/): description of the archaeological landscape, petroglyphs, settlements, and burials.

English source metadata has been summarized into Kazakh and Russian in the seed records. Those translations and wording still need review by a subject-matter editor. The approximate chronology for Tanbaly is intentionally broad.
